Link building

Link building: what it is, what it costs, how I do it safely

Link building is acquiring hyperlinks from other websites to yours. Google treats each link as a recommendation: the more relevant links from quality domains point to you, the higher you rank. I build links only from real Slovak and Czech domains with actual traffic, never from spam networks. You pay the real price the publication charges, with no markup. My work costs 12 EUR per hour.

WordPress SEO

WordPress SEO: from a slow blog to a lead machine

WordPress is SEO-ready, but only when it is set up. I fix speed (LCP under 2.5s), configure one SEO plugin (Rank Math or Yoast, never both), fix the structure (permalinks, sitemap, canonicals), add schema (Organization, Article, Product for WooCommerce), and wire internal links so every page passes equity. Typical setup is 6 to 10 hours (72 to 120 EUR at 12 EUR per hour), then 2 to 4 hours per month for content and maintenance.

SEO audit

SEO audit: 40 specific points, not a PDF for the drawer

An SEO audit is a systematic evaluation that answers three questions: why you are not succeeding in Google, what exactly needs fixing, and in what order. I start with a free initial audit delivered within 3 business days: the 10 biggest problems and opportunities on one page. A detailed audit is an action plan with hours and priorities, not a 60-page PDF. My work costs 12 EUR per hour; a detailed audit is included in the first month retainer (from 180 EUR).

Technical (15 points)
  • ✓Indexation: how many pages are in Google, how many are "Crawled, currently not indexed"
  • ✓Sitemap.xml: complete, current, no errors
  • ✓Robots.txt: blocks only what it should
  • ✓Canonicalization: correct on every page, no duplicate URLs
  • ✓Core Web Vitals: LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1
  • ✓HTTPS: valid cert, no mixed content
  • ✓Structured data: Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Article/Product
  • ✓Hreflang: correct pairs and x-default if multilingual
  • ✓Internal linking: no orphan pages, strong pages pass equity
Content and keywords (15 points)
  • ✓Title and meta description on every page, keyword and CTR-optimized
  • ✓H1 unique on every page, with main keyword
  • ✓Alt text on images, descriptive not stuffed
  • ✓Thin content: pages under 300 words without real value
  • ✓Duplications: same text on multiple URLs
  • ✓Cannibalization: two pages competing for one keyword
  • ✓E-E-A-T signals: author, date, sources, sameAs, experience
  • ✓Keyword list with search volumes from Marketing Miner
  • ✓SERP analysis: what Google actually shows for main queries
  • ✓Competitor analysis: top 3, content gap, link gap

Real result: a 300-page website where adding just 6 targeted content pages on real customer queries lifted monthly impressions by 11 000 (+14 percent) and clicks by 43 percent in the last week. This is what a good audit finds first: not 50 things to do, but the 6 that actually move the needle.
